Frequently Asked Questions

Aapke sawaalon ke asaan jawaab

1. Daily Income kab aur kaise credit hoti hai?
Har plan ki daily income har 24 ghante (raat 12:00 AM) me automatically aapke wallet balance me credit ho jati hai.
2. Minimum withdrawal amount kitna hai?
Platform par minimum withdrawal limit ₹200 hai. Aap bank account ya UPI ke madhyam se kabhi bhi payout request bhej sakte hain.
3. Withdrawal kitne samay me account me aata hai?
Withdrawal request verify hone ke baad aamtaur par 1 se 24 ghante ke andar aapke bank account me transfer kar di jaati hai.
4. Referral / Invite Commission kitna milta hai?
Level 1 par 27% direct recharge commission, Level 2 par 2% aur Level 3 par 1% commission turant balance me milta hai.
5. Recharge balance wallet me kaise add karein?
Recharge page par diye gaye QR code/UPI par payment karein aur apna 12-digit UTR / Reference number submit karein. Admin approval ke baad balance add ho jayega.

Koi aur sawal hai?

Humari official support team aapki madad ke liye 24/7 uplabdh hai.
